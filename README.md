# Ukhiya

Ukhiya is a free mahar tracker for Mahdavis. It helps you record money set aside toward
zar-e-surkh-e-khalis and follow your progress toward a mahar target.

## What you can do

- Choose a traditional target of 9, 10, or 11 ukhiya.
- Set a custom cash mahar in a supported currency.
- Record mahar savings and see your completion percentage.
- View the 24K gold-equivalent estimate for your entries.
- Review your mahar history and export your records.
- Update your target and preferences or permanently delete your account data.

## Important note

Ukhiya does not buy, sell, trade, transfer, or store gold. It is a record-keeping tool. Gold
figures are estimates based on 24K consumer prices and are not proof of physical ownership, a
payment receipt, financial advice, or an investment product.

The traditional conversion defaults are 1 ukhiya = 11 tolas and 1 tola = 11.6638 grams. You can
confirm or adjust these values during setup.

## Privacy

Your records are stored to provide the tracker. Ukhiya does not use your data for analytics,
advertising, or hidden tracking. You can export your records from Settings and delete your account
data there at any time.
